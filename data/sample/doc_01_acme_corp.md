## Executive Summary
Acme Cloud is an enterprise infrastructure provider providing distributed storage, edge compute, and database solutions. 

## Strategic Mergers & Acquisitions
In June 2024, Acme Cloud completed the strategic corporate acquisition of DataWeaver Inc. for an undisclosed sum. DataWeaver Inc. operates as a specialized data management subsidiary within Acme Cloud's Data Systems Division, retaining its principal engineering staff in Seattle, Washington.
