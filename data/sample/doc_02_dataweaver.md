## Product Portfolio
DataWeaver Inc. is the primary creator and developer of OmniMesh, an ultra-low latency in-memory distributed cache engine engineered for high-throughput transactional architectures. 

## Architectural Foundations
OmniMesh utilizes a custom lock-free ring buffer design coupled with consensus replication, enabling zero-copy network ingestion across multi-region server clusters.
