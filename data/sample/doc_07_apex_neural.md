## Corporate Overview
Apex Neural is an AI accelerator systems startup based in Cambridge, Massachusetts. The company was founded by Dr. Elena Vance following her departure from NovaGen Laboratories. Apex Neural commercializes silicon implementations built upon the licensed HyperGraph Routing Algorithm.
