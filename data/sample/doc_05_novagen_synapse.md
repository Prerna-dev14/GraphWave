## Research Initiatives
Between 2021 and 2023, the NovaGen AI Research Laboratories funded the flagship Synapse Project. The Synapse Project was led by Principal Scientist Dr. Elena Vance and focused on non-Euclidean geometric deep learning and graph neural network compilation.
