## Platform Architecture
ShieldPay is a real-time cross-border payment settlement gateway processing multi-currency transactions.

## Data Protection and Security
For encryption at rest across all transactional ledger partitions, ShieldPay integrates with KryptonVault as its designated cryptographic key management and envelope encryption engine.
